Before graduate study I worked as a **Senior Software Instructor** at Fujian Guoke Information
Technology (Nov 2019 – Oct 2023), running university courses end to end: syllabus design, lectures,
labs, assignments, exams, grading, and supervision of student projects.

Over that period I taught at roughly **eleven universities and enterprise training programmes**,
including Huaqiao University, reaching **more than 1,000 learners**. I also independently designed and
delivered a big-data training programme for **State Grid Corporation of China**, taught at their
Fuzhou headquarters to staff from across Fujian Province.

I am a certified **Huawei Instructor** (HCIA / HCIP).
